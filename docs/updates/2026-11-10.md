## Clarifying the text-control question

The TA asked whether similar control results reflect redundant news information
in the representations or limited value of the text for the target metric.
These remain research hypotheses.

The supplied meeting summary describes the code problem as text remaining in
"memory." The inspected mechanism is more specific. Price tokens attend to
article tokens before `s_vec_residual` enters the built-in TS-only prediction.
That comparison therefore uses article-conditioned price features. See the
[existing explanation and control definitions](../../analysis/text_value/README.md).

Nicole's helper derives fresh features from prices and offers two separately
defined inference interventions. Its tests establish article independence on
the tested fixture. Numerical effects on the full checkpoint remain pending.
This code finding alone does not establish pretrained-model memorization, nor
does it identify Adaeze's separate uploaded zero-text implementation.

Adaeze's uploaded overall MSE is almost unchanged after shuffling, while correct
text has about 5.9% lower error than her zero-text condition. The event matching
and text-presence comparisons therefore need separate reporting. These results
belong to her experiment and should retain that identity when used as motivation
for the next evaluation.

The next diagnostic compares correct, several seeded shuffled, and explicit
article-free inputs on fixed price examples and one verified checkpoint. Save
paired predictions to analyze event types, past volatility, and horizons. A
follow-up representation probe can be scoped after that comparison and release-
time checks. Reliable independent announcement labels would be needed to test
whether embeddings recover a specific news signal.
